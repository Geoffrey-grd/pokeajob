

## Overview

**pokeajob** is a platform designed to simplify the internship search and management process. It connects three types of users: students looking for internships, companies offering positions, and academic pilots overseeing the process.

🌐 **Live at: [http://pokeajob.francecentral.cloudapp.azure.com]**

---

## How It Works

1. **Create an account** — Sign up by choosing your profile type: Student, Company, or Pilot.
2. **Set up your profile** — Fill in the relevant information depending on your account type.
3. **Get started** — Browse offers, post positions, or supervise students depending on your role.

---

## Account Types

The platform supports three distinct account types, each with its own set of features and permissions.

---

### 🎒 Student Account

- [ ] Browse offers
- [ ] Apply to offers
- [ ] Set up a wishlist
- [ ] Build an internship tracker

---

### 🏢 Company Account

- [ ] Post and modify their offers
- [ ] Manage applications
- [ ] Browse offer

---

### 🧭 Pilot (Supervisor) Account

- [ ] Browse offers
- [ ] Access their student list
- [ ] Overview their student's internship tracker
